Associate Product Manager, Growth
Kiranaly (quick commerce, Bengaluru)

About the role
Kiranaly delivers groceries in 15 minutes across Bengaluru. You will own one growth funnel end to end, from first app open to second order.

What you will do
- Find the biggest drop-offs in the new-user funnel and fix them with engineering and design.
- Design, run and read A/B tests.
- Talk to customers every week and turn what you hear into product decisions.
- Write clear PRDs that engineers can build from.
- Track conversion and retention, and report weekly to the growth lead.

Requirements (must have)
- 1 to 3 years in product, growth, analytics or operations.
- SQL for your own analysis.
- Experience running experiments or A/B tests.
- Strong written communication, including PRDs or specs.
- Experience working with engineers or a product team.

Nice to have
- Mixpanel or Amplitude.
- Experience with a consumer app or D2C brand.
- Kannada or Hindi, for customer calls.
