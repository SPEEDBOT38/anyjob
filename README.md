# AnyJob

Paste your CV and one job post. AnyJob says whether to apply, rewrites the CV toward the job honestly, and lets you approve every change. Tex, the mascot, is a resume page that rewrites one line at a time.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser. Add a Gemini API key in Settings (it stays in your browser's localStorage, never in the file).

One file, no build step: HTML, CSS and JavaScript in `index.html`, with mammoth.js (read .docx) and docx (write .docx) from CDNs, per `Apply-or-Not_Student-Pack/BUILD_SPEC.md`.

## What it does

1. **Your fit.** Every requirement in the job post, must-haves first, each with a quote from your CV. Code checks every quote is really in your CV, then scores it (must 2 points, nice 1) and gives a verdict.
2. **What your CV leaves out.** Up to three questions about the gaps. Only your true answers are used.
3. **Review and approve.** Before and proposed CV side by side, editable, with a list of changes. Nothing is saved until you tick "every line is true" and approve.
4. **Your approved CV.** Before and after scores, rounds, and an honesty check that flags every name and number not in your CV or your answers. Download as Word or LaTeX once every flag is resolved.

Applications are saved in the browser and listed on Home and Applications.

## Design

`design/` holds the FigCraft files and PNG exports, from wireframes to the final Signal direction (`design/anyjob-signal-app.figcraft`, `design/screens-signal/`). The screens are generated by the scripts in `design/tools/`. Tex lives in `design/mascot/tex.html`.
